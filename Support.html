Getting started

Do I need to create an account?

No. Desca has no sign-up and no login. Open the app and everything is ready to use right away.

Does Desca work without an internet connection?

Yes, for almost everything. Notes, timers, alarms, goals, and Apple Intelligence AI features all work fully offline. The only feature that needs a one-time internet connection is downloading the optional on-device AI model, and restoring a previous purchase.

AI features

Why does it say "AI unavailable"?

This means Apple Intelligence isn't turned on for your device and the optional local AI model hasn't been downloaded yet. Go to Settings → On-Device AI in Desca, or turn on Apple Intelligence in your iPhone's own Settings app, then tap the refresh icon in Desca.

Does my note content get sent anywhere?

No. Whichever AI path is used, Apple Intelligence or the local model, your note text is processed entirely on your device and never leaves it.

Why is the AI model download so large?

The on-device model is around 475MB, since it's a full language model running locally rather than a lightweight call to a server. We'd recommend downloading it on Wi-Fi.

Timers & alarms

My alarm didn't ring. What happened?

Check that notifications are allowed for Desca in your iPhone's Settings app, and that Focus/Do Not Disturb isn't set to silence alarms. Desca uses Apple's own AlarmKit system to ring alarms, the same system Apple's Clock app uses, so if Clock alarms work on your phone, Desca's will too.

A Live Activity or Dynamic Island timer got stuck.

Go to Settings → Your Data → "Reset stuck timers/widgets" in Desca. This clears any leftover Live Activity without affecting your saved notes, goals, or history.

Notes & scanning

Can I add photos to a note?

Yes, tap the photo icon in the note editor. This uses the system photo picker, so Desca never gets broader access to your photo library, just the specific photo you choose.

Where do my flashcards go after I generate them?

Flashcards are filed under the note's subject. Review them from the Timer tab's Focus section by selecting that subject.

Purchases & subscriptions

How do I cancel Mythic Pass?

Subscriptions are managed entirely through Apple, not inside Desca. Open your iPhone's Settings app → your name → Subscriptions → Mythic Pass, and cancel from there.

I bought a theme on my old phone. How do I get it back?

Purchases are tied to your Apple ID. Open Settings in Desca and look for a "Restore Purchases" option, or reinstall the app while signed into the same Apple ID you originally purchased with.

I was charged but don't see my purchase unlocked.

Try Restore Purchases first, and make sure you're connected to the internet. If it still isn't showing up, email us with your Apple order receipt and we'll help sort it out.

Data & backups

How do I back up my notes before switching phones?

Go to Settings → Your Data → "Export my data." This saves a file you can AirDrop or save to Files, then use "Import data" on your new device to bring everything back in.

Will deleting the app delete my data?

Yes. Since everything is stored only on your device, uninstalling Desca removes your notes, flashcards, and goals along with it. Export a backup first if you want to keep a copy.

Troubleshooting

The app feels slow or a screen won't load.

Try fully closing Desca (swipe it away from the App Switcher) and reopening it. If a specific AI response seems stuck, wait a few seconds first, on-device generation can take a moment depending on your device.

Notifications aren't showing up.

Check iPhone Settings → Notifications → Desca, and make sure Allow Notifications is on. Also check the individual toggles inside Desca's own Settings screen, since each notification type (goal check-ins, streaks, reminders) can be turned on or off separately.
